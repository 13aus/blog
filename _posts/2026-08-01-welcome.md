This is where my offensive security notes move out of Obsidian and into
the open. Writing a technique up clearly is the best test of whether I
actually understand it.

What you'll find here:

- **Machine writeups** from HackTheBox, TryHackMe, and HackSmarter, covering
  full methodology including the dead ends, not just the flag
- **CPTS study notes:** Active Directory attack chains, pivoting, MSSQL abuse
- **Research:** CVE breakdowns when the rabbit hole is deep enough to document
- **Tooling:** scripts and tool notes when something is worth sharing

All machine writeups are for retired boxes only.
